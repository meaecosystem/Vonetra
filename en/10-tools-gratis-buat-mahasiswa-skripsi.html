10 Free Tools for Thesis Writing — 100% Safe, No Ads

Working on your thesis and tired of online tools that slap on watermarks, demand an account, or suddenly ask you to upgrade? Here are 10 tools that genuinely come in handy for everyday thesis work — all free, running right in your browser without uploading files to any server, and with no ads breaking your concentration.

No need to worry about your thesis data leaking

One common worry when using online tools for important documents: where does the file get sent, and where does it end up stored? With every tool above, the processing happens directly in your own browser — not uploaded to a server and sent back.
