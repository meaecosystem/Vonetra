Steps to extract colors

  1. Open tools.meaecosystem.com/color-picker.
  2. Upload the image whose colors you want to pick.
  3. Click the part of the image you want — the Hex, RGB, and HSL codes for that spot appear instantly and can be copied with one click.
  4. To see the overall dominant color palette, scroll to the palette section and choose how many colors to display (4, 8, or 16).

Hex, RGB, or HSL — what's the difference?

All three represent the same color, just written in different formats, and each is more common in certain contexts. Hex (like #3B82F6) is the most common in CSS and web design. RGB separates the red, green, and blue components — handy when the software you're using needs separate numeric inputs. HSL (hue, saturation, lightness) is more intuitive when you want to adjust a color's brightness or saturation without changing its "type".

When to use direct clicking vs. the automatic palette?

Direct clicking works better when you already know exactly which part of the image you want the color from — a logo color or a specific product color, for example. The automatic palette is more useful early on, like when you're just starting to work out a color scheme from a reference photo or moodboard.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The image is read and processed directly in your browser using the Canvas API.

Which image formats are supported?

All common image formats your browser can read, such as JPG, PNG, and WebP.

Why does the extracted color look different from what I see in the image?

Your screen and its brightness settings can affect how you perceive a color visually, but the extracted color code is still based on the actual pixel values in the image file, not your screen's display.
