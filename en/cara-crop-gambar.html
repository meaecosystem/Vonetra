How to Crop an Image to the Right Ratio

Want to use a photo for a square Instagram post, a 16:9 YouTube cover, or a 9:16 story, but the image isn't quite the right ratio yet? Here's how to crop it right in your browser.

Steps to crop an image

  1. Open tools.meaecosystem.com/image-cropper.
  2. Upload the image you want to crop.
  3. Choose a ratio: Free (no restriction), 1:1 (square), 4:3, 16:9, 9:16, or 3:2, depending on what you need.
  4. Drag and adjust the crop area directly in the preview until the part you want fits inside the box.
  5. Click "Crop Image", then download the result.

Which ratio suits which platform?

A 1:1 (square) ratio is commonly used for Instagram feed posts. A 16:9 ratio suits video thumbnails or YouTube covers. A 9:16 ratio fits vertical content like Stories or Reels. 4:3 and 3:2 ratios are more commonly used for standard photos. Check the recommended ratio for your target platform before cropping, so the result isn't automatically re-cropped when you upload it.

Tips for choosing a good crop area

Make sure the most important part of the image (a face, the main subject, text) stays within the crop area and isn't too close to the edge — some platforms trim a small portion of the edges when displaying previews or thumbnails.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The cropping process runs directly in your browser.

Does image quality drop after cropping?

Cropping itself doesn't reduce the quality of the remaining image — you're only trimming away the part you don't use. The quality of the remaining part stays exactly the same as the original.

Can I change the ratio after starting to crop?

Yes, you can switch the ratio choice at any time before clicking the crop button — the crop area will adjust automatically.
