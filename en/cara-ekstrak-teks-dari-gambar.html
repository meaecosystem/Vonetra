How to Extract Text from a Photo or Image (OCR)

Have a screenshot, a scanned page, or a photo of a whiteboard that you need typed out? Instead of retyping it by hand, let OCR read the writing and turn it into text you can copy.

October 1, 2026 · 2 min read

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is technology that recognizes writing in an image and turns it into digital text you can copy, edit, or search. This tool processes it directly in your browser and can handle many images at once — the results are merged into a single text file.

Steps to extract text

  1. Open tools.meaecosystem.com/image-to-text.
  2. Upload one or more images that contain text.
  3. Choose the language of the text in the image: Indonesian, English, or Indonesian + English if the image mixes both.
  4. Wait for the OCR process to finish. For multiple images at once, the results are automatically merged into one tidy text file.
  5. Copy or download the resulting text.

Choose the right language for accurate results

Picking the language that matches the image's content matters for accuracy — the OCR engine uses specific language dictionaries and patterns to guess ambiguous characters. If the image only contains Indonesian text, picking "Indonesian" alone usually gives more accurate results than picking a combined option.

What kind of image gives the most accurate results?

Images with clear text, high contrast between the writing and background, and no tilt or blur usually produce the most accurate OCR results — such as a screenshot of digital text or a neatly scanned document. Photos of handwriting, tilted text, or photos with poor lighting tend to produce more reading errors.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The OCR process runs directly in your browser.

Is there a limit on the number of images I can process?

There's no limit from the service side. Processing time depends on the number of images and your device's specs.

Why are some words misread?

OCR isn't always 100% accurate, especially with low-quality images, unusual fonts, or handwriting. It's a good idea to double-check the results, especially for anything requiring high precision.
