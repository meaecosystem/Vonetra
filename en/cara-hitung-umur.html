How to Calculate Exact Age and the Difference Between Two Dates

Want to know your exact age down to the month and day, or how much time is left until a certain date? Here's how to calculate it with no manual counting on a calendar.

October 1, 2026 · 2 min read

Age Calculator precisely computes the difference between two dates — not just years, but the remaining months and days too. It can be used to calculate age from a birth date up to today, or the difference between any two dates, like how long it's been since an event or how many days remain until a specific date.

Steps to calculate age

  1. Open tools.meaecosystem.com/age-calculator.
  2. Enter a birth date (or the start date to calculate from) in the first field.
  3. Enter the date to calculate up to in the second field — defaults to today, but can be changed to any date.
  4. The result appears instantly, as a precise difference in years, months, and days.

Why isn't the result just "X years"?

Stating age in years alone is often imprecise — two people who are both "25 years old" could differ by nearly a full year depending on their birth dates. By calculating down to months and days, the result becomes far more accurate, useful for administrative purposes that need an exact age, or just out of curiosity about exactly how many days you've been alive.

Useful for more than just calculating age

Its use isn't limited to birthdays — you can also use it to calculate the difference between any two dates. For instance, how long it's been since you started your current job, or how many days remain until a wedding date or a particular deadline.

Frequently asked questions

Are the dates I enter sent to a server?

No. The calculation is done directly in your browser.

Does it account for leap years?

Yes, the date calculation accounts for the actual number of days in each month and year, including leap years.

Can it calculate the difference for a future date?

Yes. This tool can calculate the difference between any two dates, whether in the past or the future.
