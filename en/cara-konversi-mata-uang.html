Steps to convert currency

  1. Open tools.meaecosystem.com/unit-converter-mata-uang.
  2. Choose the source currency in the "From" field and the target currency in the "To" field.
  3. Type the amount to convert — the result appears instantly.
  4. Note the "Rate as of [month year]" label shown on the page — it indicates when the rate was last updated.

When is this tool a good fit, and when isn't it?

It's a good fit for getting a rough idea or estimated rate — say, while researching the price of a foreign product or just out of curiosity about an exchange rate. But for needs that require precise, up-to-the-minute figures — bank transfers, exchanging money at a money changer, or official financial documents — always check the rate directly from a bank or a trusted financial service provider, since market rates change constantly, sometimes even hour to hour.

Why does my result differ from what I see in other apps?

Every provider — banks, apps, money changers — has their own rate and margin that changes daily, even hourly. This tool uses a fixed reference rate until it's manually updated, so a gap with the live rate elsewhere is normal, not a sign something's wrong.

Frequently asked questions

Is the rate here the same as a bank's rate?

No. The rate in this tool is a reference rate updated manually on a periodic basis, not an official bank rate that changes in real time.

Why doesn't the rate update automatically?

So this tool can keep running 100% in the browser with no extra server pulling rate data live.

How do I know how long it's been since the rate was updated?

Check the "Rate as of [month year]" label shown on the tool's page. That label shows the period the reference rate was last updated.
