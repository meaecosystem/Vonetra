Meet MEA Link — One Profile for Links, a Résumé, Portfolio, and Business Card

If you need one place to put every important link in your social media bio, plus a résumé people can view without downloading, a portfolio to showcase your work, and a digital business card — MEA Link brings all of that together into one platform, usable from a single account.

October 3, 2026 · 1 min read

1. Link Bio — A Set of Links on One Page

The most basic feature: a page holding a set of links you can put in your Instagram, TikTok, or other platform bio. Accessed at mealink.my.id/your-username.

2. Online Résumé — No Need to Send a PDF File

Instead of sending a résumé as a PDF attachment that sometimes looks messy when opened on another device, your résumé can be viewed directly through a link, with no download needed first. Accessed at mealink.my.id/cv/your-username.

3. Portfolio — No Coding Needed

For anyone who wants to showcase their work but doesn't have the time or skill to build their own website, there's a no-code portfolio option — just pick a theme, fill in a form, done. Accessed at mealink.my.id/p/your-username.

4. Digital Business Card — Replacing a Physical Card

A digital business card complete with a QR code, ready to be shared or scanned by someone else to save your contact details and social media links all at once. Accessed at mealink.my.id/cb/your-username.

One Account, Every Feature

Managed from One Dashboard

All four are managed from the same dashboard at link.meaecosystem.com, complete with an analytics page to see how often each of your profiles gets viewed or clicked. Some features are already free to use, while others are still in development and will keep being added going forward.
