Vonetra Is Now Vonetra

The blog you're reading right now has officially been renamed to Vonetra, and moved to its new domain. A pure name and domain change -- the content, purpose, and every existing article stay exactly the same.

October 24, 2026 · 1 min read
Product Status

Fully Live

Vonetra is already fully running on its new domain. All existing articles stay accessible as normal -- only the name and domain have changed.

Open Vonetra →

Why the name change?

In line with the rebranding of other products under MEA Ecosystem, the name "Vonetra" was replaced so it could have a stronger identity of its own, moving away from the "MEA + function" pattern used before. Unlike some other products whose rebrand also changed their core features (such as Ordevo), Vonetra's rebrand is purely a name and domain change -- no shift in concept at all.

New domain: vonetra.meaecosystem.com

This blog is now accessed through this domain:

vonetra.meaecosystem.com

The old domain (blog.meaecosystem.com) will gradually be redirected to this new one.

What doesn't change

Vonetra's function stays the same as when it was still called Vonetra -- a home for internal articles about MEA Ecosystem's products and general tech topics, available in 5 languages (Indonesian, Japanese, English, Chinese, Korean). Vonetra isn't a real-time news portal -- that's Newsolva's (formerly MEA News) role.

The MEA Ecosystem name and logo as the parent brand also stay the same.

Summary

Vonetra has officially been renamed to Vonetra and has fully moved to the domain vonetra.meaecosystem.com. A pure name and domain change, with no shift in concept or features.
