MEA Study Is Now Scholavi

MEA Study, the learning and knowledge-search hub under MEA Ecosystem, has officially been renamed to Scholavi. It's already accessible now on its new domain, even as parts of it keep getting refined.

October 22, 2026 · 1 min read
Product Status

Live, Still Being Developed

Scholavi is already accessible now on its new domain. The Library feature is already running, and more features will keep rolling out and getting refined going forward.

Open Scholavi →

Why the name change?

In line with the rebranding of other products under MEA Ecosystem, the name "MEA Study" was replaced so it could have a stronger identity of its own, moving away from the "MEA + function" pattern used before. The name Scholavi was chosen to reflect its role as a space for learning and exploring knowledge.

New domain: scholavi.meaecosystem.com

Scholavi is now accessed through this domain:

scholavi.meaecosystem.com

The old domain will gradually be redirected to this new one.

What does Scholavi do?

Scholavi functions as a learning platform. Where Synoltra (formerly MEA Hub) focuses on productivity, Scholavi positions itself the other way around: focused on learning.

Features already running right now:

  • Library — connected directly to the Lorestra engine (the same engine that powers Ensiklo), so its content automatically updates as Lorestra develops.
  • Classes — structured learning spaces, still being actively developed.

Other features such as practice exercises and digital signatures for important documents (like a thesis) are still on the roadmap for later development.

What doesn't change

The MEA Ecosystem name and logo as the parent brand stay exactly the same. Only this product's own name and identity are changing.

Summary

MEA Study has officially been renamed to Scholavi and is already accessible at scholavi.meaecosystem.com. The Library (connected to Lorestra) is already running, Classes are still being developed, and practice exercises plus document digital signatures remain on the roadmap.
