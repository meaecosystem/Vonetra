Siesta Updated to Paper v1.1 on Zenodo

The Siesta research paper published last September has just been bumped a version, from v1.0 to v1.1, on Zenodo. Several things that were still open items in the first version now have progress to show.

October 3, 2026 · 1 min read

One of the changes is in the Michi component, the part whose job is detecting the language a user is using before their message gets passed on to Siesta. Its scope was adjusted — from the original plan of supporting dozens of languages, it's now focused on 14 languages — and pretraining itself is now complete, after its status was still empty in the previous version.

Meanwhile, development of a far larger-scale model — the v6 generation with over 4 billion parameters — is still ongoing, and the process hasn't been without friction. There are still a few technical hurdles around context-size configuration and how to split the computational load across multiple TPU chips at once that need resolving first.
